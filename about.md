## I break things so companies don't have to find out the hard way

Hi, I'm Keerthan — a penetration tester who spent six months getting paid to think like an attacker across 10+ client engagements. I learned that most breaches don't come from zero-days. They come from the boring stuff: a reused password, a misconfigured endpoint, an IDOR nobody bothered to check. Finding that boring stuff before someone with worse intentions does — that's the job.

I'm currently pursuing an MSc in Cyber Security at the University of Hertfordshire, while contributing to bug bounty programs and doing independent security research on the side. Outside of that, I build my own vulnerable machines from scratch (deliberately broken SSH, FTP, MySQL, Apache — the works) just to have a sandbox nobody else has already solved. I also trained a Random Forest classifier to catch malware hiding in IoT firmware, hit 92% accuracy, and got it published — because sometimes the interesting problems aren't on the OWASP Top 10.

This blog is where the CTF machines live. Not polished tutorials — actual notes from actual attempts, including the wrong turns.

Web app pentesting, API security, Active Directory attacks, and turning a five-line nmap scan into a full compromise chain.
